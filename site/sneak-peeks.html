Sneak peek 01

Movaryn: Phase 2

In design · No release date

Phase 2 targets the two things that slow a kit down most: the model not knowing who you are talking about, and the wait before a useful answer appears.

Research before invention

Mentioning an obscure anime or manga character should not make a model pattern-match a name and invent abilities. Phase 2 adds an explicit research step before the first draft:

  • An optional lookup checks the character's series, powers, weapons and silhouette, and shows where each fact came from.
  • If the character cannot be verified, Movaryn says so and asks for the series or a short description instead of guessing.
  • Follow canon and Original kit are separate paths: one respects known abilities, the other uses the design as a starting point and labels what was invented.
  • A character brief is saved with the project, so a later chat does not re-litigate who the character is.
  • Research is opt-in and server-side: no silent web requests, no key in the browser, and the app still works with no lookup configured.

A faster path to the first move

  • Shorter setup before the kit, with no restating the brief back at you.
  • The first move streams before the rest of the answer is finished, so something usable is on screen sooner.
  • A Quick kit path drafts one coherent pass first, then offers depth on request instead of opening with an essay.
  • Clear progress states while a lookup runs, so waiting has a reason and an end.

What Phase 2 will not do

  • Invent canon for a character it cannot verify.
  • Scrape or upload anything silently.
  • Promise a date before the work is real.

The roadmap is updated as this moves.

Sneak peek 02

Movaryn: Character briefs

In design · No release date

Today each chat starts from whatever the model thinks it remembers, so a name can mean one thing in the first reply and something else after a restart. A character brief makes that understanding explicit, project-scoped and editable.

One brief per character

  • A short brief records the series, which version of the character is meant, the abilities worth keeping and the ones to avoid.
  • Follow canon, Original kit and Mixed are stated modes, not an unspoken assumption. The mode appears on the kit, so a reader can tell what was respected and what was invented.
  • The brief is captured before drafting, and every later reply in the project reuses it — including after a restart or a new chat.
  • Every line the model wrote can be edited or replaced. The brief belongs to the project, and the app never quietly overwrites it.

Where the facts come from

  • Facts are labelled: carried in the brief, taken from a lookup with a source, or invented for an original kit.
  • Switching from Follow canon to Original kit keeps the brief and starts a fresh draft instead of rewriting the earlier one.
  • If the brief and a lookup disagree, Movaryn shows the conflict and asks which one wins rather than picking silently.
  • No brief is required to start. Without one, the app asks the questions it would otherwise guess at.

What this will not do

  • Treat a lookup as canon when it cannot cite a source.
  • Lock a project to one interpretation forever.
  • Hide the canon-or-original choice inside a prompt you cannot see.

The roadmap tracks this work alongside Phase 2.
