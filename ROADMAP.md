# Movaryn roadmap

A living document. Scope and order can change, and nothing here is a promise of a date.

## Now

- **More moveset sparks** — per-game archetypes and starter kits, not only generic prompts.
- **Reorder saved moves** — drag a project's moves into combo order instead of adding them in chat order.
- **Side-by-side kits** — put two replies next to each other to compare roles, cooldowns and counterplay.

## Next

- **Per-project art memory** — keep a project's style, palette and references attached to its art brief.
- **Printable project sheet** — one export that includes the brief, moves, art prompts and kit check.
- **Provider presets** — pick OpenAI, OpenRouter, Ollama or the Freebuff API from Settings instead of typing endpoints.
- **Palette search for saved art** — find a saved image by its prompt without opening the project drawer.

## Later

- **Balance scorecards** — local, explainable comparisons of damage, range and commitment across a kit.
- **Reference library** — reusable outfit and silhouette references shared across projects.
- **More games and formats** — templates for battleground games beyond JJS and TSB.
- **Localisation** — interface and prompt translations that keep constraints intact.
